Work With Us

We are looking for talented researchers who are ready to tackle life sciences challenges. If you are passionate about high-impact science and thrive in a collaborative environment, we invite you to explore the opportunities within our group.

Postdoctoral Opportunities

We welcome informal enquiries from potential postdoctoral candidates with outstanding research track records. We are particularly interested in supporting those prepared to apply for independent funding through fellowships such as:


  • EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowships
  • BBSRC Discovery Fellowships
  • Marie Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships
  • HFSP Postdoctoral Fellowships
  • 1851 Research Fellowship
  • Dorothy Hodgkin Fellowship

If you have a strong background and a clear research vision, we will provide full assistance in developing a competitive proposal.

PhD & MSc Studentships

Students interested in pursuing postgraduate research or summer internships are encouraged to reach out.

  • Application Process: Most students join us through established scholarship or training programmes, such as:
    • SWBio DTP
    • GW4 BioMed DTP
    • CDT in SCT
  • Requirements: Applicants should hold, or expect to achieve, a minimum 1st or 2:1 degree (or equivalent) in Chemistry, Biochemistry, or a closely related field.
  • International Applicants: Please note that you must be able to secure independent funding, such as through a government bursary or external scholarship.
